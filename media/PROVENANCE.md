# Asset provenance

The published site uses only pre-rendered WebP images of the supplied ring. No GLB, GLTF, STL, OBJ, Blender file or other 3D mesh is included in public/ or the static export.

- snap-logo.webp: provided SNAP bitmap; outer whitespace crop and web-size compression only. No redraw or recoloring.
- sequence/base/*.webp: Blender Cycles renders of the supplied polished model, 25 camera-relative angles, transparent background.
- sequence/block/*.webp and sequence/double/*.webp: renders of the same ring with explicitly fictional demonstration attachments. Not a released assortment or production-fit specification.
- poster.webp, hero.webp, side.webp, inside.webp: local Blender product renders; not photos of a manufactured metal product.
- Golos Text: locally bundled OFL font from @fontsource-variable/golos-text.

The private Blender source and rendering tools remain outside the deliverable. Published images can still be downloaded and used as visual references; the site does not transmit the model's mesh.
